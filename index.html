Features

Built for serious collectors

From the first shutter click to a fully documented album — every tool you need to identify, value, and journal your coins.

Dual-side photo capture

Photograph both obverse and reverse. AI uses both faces for sharper identification and variety detection.

Coin categories

US Modern, Classic, World, Ancient, Error/Variety, and Tokens — structured coverage for every tray.

Ranked matches + confidence

See multiple candidates ordered by confidence score so you can verify — not just accept the first guess.

Free Gemini, upgrade anytime

Start free with Gemini. One-tap upgrade to Pro, GPT-4o, or Claude when you need deeper analysis.

Honest value ranges

Reference-only price ranges — transparent estimates, never presented as appraisals or guarantees.

Collection journal

Notes, condition grades, purchase price, and photos — a living album for every piece you own.

Full export & share

Export your collection data and share findings with fellow collectors, dealers, or your future self.

Auto-upgrade on low confidence

When confidence is low, optionally re-run with a stronger model — better IDs when the coin is tough.

How it Works

Three steps to clarity

From unknown pocket change to a documented entry in your album.

1

Capture both sides

Snap the obverse, then flip for the reverse. Good light and a steady hand go a long way.

2

Review ranked matches

AI returns candidates with confidence scores, categories, and honest value ranges for reference.

3

Save to your journal

Add condition, purchase price, and personal notes. Export or share whenever you need.
